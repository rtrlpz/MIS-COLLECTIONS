## Analysis Based on the Role

### Collections Director cares about:
- Overall portfolio health (delinquency rates, roll rates, cure rates)
- Recovery rates by product, by month, by segment
- Cost-to-collect (operational efficiency)
- Agent productivity benchmarks
- PTP performance trends
- Compliance metrics (call attempts, contact rates, regulatory adherence)
- Month-over-month performance, targets vs actuals
- Escalation patterns, early warning indicators

### Managers care about:
- Team performance comparisons
- Agent rankings, top/bottom performers
- Campaign effectiveness (which strategies work)
- PTP kept vs broken by team
- Contact rates, RPC rates by team
- Handle time efficiency
- Queue management, workload distribution

### Supervisors care about:
- Daily agent activity logs
- Real-time (or near-real-time) performance
- Coaching opportunities (underperformers)
- Individual agent metrics (AHT, utilization, RPC, PTP kept rate)
- Exception handling, escalations
- Schedule adherence, attendance impact on performance
